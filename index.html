Bio

Evgeny Lyandres’ research focuses on the interaction between firms’ financial and operating strategies, and on the effects of product market competition on firms’ financial decisions, such as capital structure choices, M&A strategies and going-public choices. He is also interested in applications of real options theory to firms’ financial and investment decisions. Recently, his research focus has shifted largely to applications of blockchain technology in finance and to Decentralized Finance (DeFi). His theoretical and empirical papers have been published in leading finance journals, such as the Journal of Finance, Journal of Financial Economics, Review of Financial Studies, Management Science, Journal of Business, Review of Finance, and Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis, among many others, and have received numerous awards and prizes. Evgeny serves as a Co-editor of the Journal of Corporate Finance and as an Associate Editor of several journals. He teaches courses on blockchain and DeFi at the BA, MSc, executive education, and PhD levels on four continents.
