Networks of misconduct: Social media networks and asset prices in cryptocurrency markets

with Charis Eleftheriou, Demetris Koursaros, Christodoulos Louca, Roni Michaely
working paper

WP

We construct a user-level measure of enforcement exposure in cryptocurrency markets based on each user’s network proximity to accounts previously linked to SEC enforcement or investigative matters. The measure identifies at-risk users well in advance of enforcement action and predicts future SEC enforcement. Users with high scores employ more promotional language, urgency and pressure cues, and occupy more central positions in networks containing influential and automated accounts. Their net positive communication clusters in short-lived market episodes of elevated trading activity and price appreciation beginning before the posts followed by rapid reversal, alongside temporarily elevated volatility. The findings show that regulatory risk is embedded in social-network topology and manifests in economically meaningful market dynamics.
